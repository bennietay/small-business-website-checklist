# Small Business Website Launch Checklist

Use this checklist with your web partner. Write down the owner and due date for each incomplete item.

## 1. Plan around a customer decision

- [ ] State what the business offers, who it serves, and where it operates.
- [ ] Choose the main action: enquiry, booking, quotation, call, visit, or purchase.
- [ ] List customer questions about price, service area, process, timing, and support.
- [ ] Gather approved business facts, genuine testimonials, real project images, and current contact routes.
- [ ] Agree who owns the domain, hosting, website files, analytics, forms, and updates.
- [ ] Set a first-release scope and defer optional features that do not support the main customer journey.

## 2. Check content and page structure

- [ ] The homepage explains the offer near the top of the page.
- [ ] Important services have a clear explanation, intended customer, practical details, and next step.
- [ ] About and contact information is accurate and current.
- [ ] Claims, qualifications, prices, guarantees, reviews, and case studies can be substantiated.
- [ ] Each page has one clear purpose and useful calls to action.
- [ ] Images are licensed for use and informative images have meaningful alternative text.

## 3. Test the mobile experience

- [ ] Review at a narrow phone width and on at least one physical iOS or Android phone.
- [ ] Navigation opens, closes, and reaches the correct pages.
- [ ] Text is readable without zooming and controls are comfortable to tap.
- [ ] Forms work with a phone keyboard and show useful errors and confirmation.
- [ ] Telephone, email, map, booking, and messaging links open the intended destination.
- [ ] Tables, images, banners, and pop-ups do not hide content or force horizontal scrolling.

## 4. Cover search basics

- [ ] Important pages have distinct titles, accurate descriptions, and logical headings.
- [ ] URLs are readable and stable; related pages link to one another where useful.
- [ ] Canonicals point to preferred URLs and hostname/protocol variants resolve consistently.
- [ ] Sitemap and robots rules match the pages intended for search.
- [ ] Search Console is verified and the sitemap is submitted.
- [ ] Structured data describes only accurate facts visible on the page.

## 5. Review performance, security, and accessibility

- [ ] Check key mobile pages with a performance tool; resize oversized images and remove unnecessary scripts.
- [ ] HTTPS works and forms send information to the intended recipient.
- [ ] Keyboard focus is visible and key journeys can be completed without a mouse.
- [ ] Text and controls have sufficient contrast and links are not distinguished only by colour.
- [ ] Form fields have labels and accessible error messages.
- [ ] Privacy notices match the site’s actual data practices.

## 6. Verify enquiries and conversions

- [ ] Submit each form and confirm delivery to the correct inbox or system.
- [ ] Check confirmation messages, thank-you pages, reply expectations, and spam handling.
- [ ] Test phone, email, messaging, booking, map, payment, and download links.
- [ ] Ask someone unfamiliar with the business to find a service and make an enquiry.
- [ ] Track important actions with a setup that matches the privacy notice.
- [ ] Remove placeholder text, demo content, and test accounts.

## 7. Keep the launch controlled

- [ ] Save a backup and confirm the rollback path before replacing the old site.
- [ ] Map changed URLs to their closest equivalent and test permanent redirects.
- [ ] Check that important old links do not lead to avoidable 404 pages.
- [ ] Recheck the live homepage, services, forms, mobile navigation, and contact details.
- [ ] Record the launch date, owner, renewal dates, backup schedule, and support contact.

## 8. Improve the first month

- [ ] Confirm analytics and enquiry tracking, then save a baseline.
- [ ] Review Search Console for queries, indexing issues, and pages that need attention.
- [ ] Ask new leads what they needed to know before contacting the business.
- [ ] Fix the highest-friction page or step, then observe before making another large change.
- [ ] Schedule regular checks for backups, updates, forms, links, and page speed.
